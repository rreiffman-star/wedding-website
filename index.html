Travel & Stay

Getting to the lagoon

By air

Marco Polo (VCE)

Venice’s main airport sits just across the lagoon. Treviso (TSF) is a smaller alternative an hour away.

Onward

Arrive by water

Water taxis reach the city from the airport in about half an hour; the Alilaguna ferry is a slower, scenic route.

Once here

On foot & by boat

There are no cars in Venice. The city is yours to walk, with vaporetto water-buses tracing the Grand Canal.

Where to stay

A few of the neighbourhoods we love. Venice fills quickly in October, so we suggest booking early.

San Marco

The grand heart of the city — minutes from the Piazza, the palazzi and the water’s edge. Storied and central.

Dorsoduro

Artful and unhurried — galleries, students and golden-hour spritzes along the Zattere. Our quiet favourite.

Cannaregio

Local, lively and lined with canalside bacari. Where Venetians actually live, and dinner runs late.
